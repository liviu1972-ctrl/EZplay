# How the Platform Works — EZPlay

Welcome to the EZPlay digital platform! This guide will show you how to navigate the platform from your first visit to managing your dashboard.

---

## 1. Landing Page (The Entrypoint)
When you first open EZPlay, you see the landing page. It presents:
*   **The Hero Area**: Explains what EZPlay is and has buttons to "Get Started" or "Learn More".
*   **The Founder Loop**: Explains the 4-step educational cycle (Business Run, Founder Debrief, Business Challenge, Level Up).
*   **The Abilites**: Displays the 5 perspectives of a business leader.
*   **Clickable Motherboard Components**: Decorative parts you can click to read more information about business processes (like decision-making and vision).

---

## 2. Creating Your Account (Sign-Up / Login)
To participate in the games, you need to create an account:
1.  Click the **"Get Started"** or **"Register"** button.
2.  You can sign up using your email and a password, or simply click **"Sign in with Google"** for a one-click login.
3.  Logging in is secure and remembers your session.

---

## 3. The 3-Step Profile Setup (Onboarding)
The first time you log in, you will be guided through a quick 3-step setup to customize your experience:
*   **Step 1: Profile Details** — Tell us your name and a brief description of yourself.
*   **Step 2: Interests** — Choose which of the 5 perspectives (Market, Product, Operations, Finance, Strategy) you want to learn most.
*   **Step 3: Profile Picture** — Upload your picture or let the app automatically generate a badge using your name initials.

---

## 4. The Dashboard
Once onboarding is complete, you are redirected to the Dashboard:
*   **Header**: Greets you with your name and displays your current role (e.g., "Builder").
*   **Token Balances**: Displays how many Coins (EZC) and Gems (EZG) you currently have.
*   **Skill Level Overview**: Displays a chart showing your progress across the 5 perspectives.
*   **Settings Page**: Allows you to edit your profile, toggle between Light and Dark mode, or switch the website language between Romanian and English.
