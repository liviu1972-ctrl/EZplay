# Roles and Permissions — EZPlay

The EZPlay platform supports multiple user roles, each tailored to a specific participant in the educational ecosystem.

---

## 1. Student Roles

### Builder (Base Level)
Every new user starts as a **Builder**. Builders are learners who participate in the educational simulations.
*   **What they can do**:
    *   Sign in and complete their profile onboarding.
    *   Access the student dashboard.
    *   View their own token balances (EZC / EZG) and skill radar chart.
    *   Edit their profile settings (display name, bio, language, theme).
    *   **View and search public card decks (`/cards`)** to study card requirements and effects.
    *   Join active game sessions (Phase 2+).

### Founder (Advanced Level)
A Builder who achieves Level 5 in all 5 perspectives (Market, Product, Operations, Finance, Strategy) is promoted to **Founder**.
*   **What they can do**:
    *   All privileges of a Builder.
    *   Unlock advanced business run challenges.
    *   Access a special badge on their profile representing their achievement.

---

## 2. Facilitator & Organizer Roles

### Facilitator (Teachers / Hosts)
Facilitators are coaches who host physical or digital business games.
*   **What they can do**:
    *   Create and manage active game sessions.
    *   Award Coins (EZC) and Gems (EZG) to players.
    *   View participant performance stats.

### Organizer (Project Managers)
Organizers are responsible for regional tournaments or schools.
*   **What they can do**:
    *   All privileges of a Facilitator.
    *   Manage multiple sessions and facilitators in their region.

---

## 3. Mentor & Admin Roles

### Mentor (Advisors)
Mentors are real entrepreneurs who provide feedback to Builders during the "Founder Debrief" sessions.
*   **What they can do**:
    *   Review Builder profiles.
    *   Provide feedback comments on player performance.

### Admin (System Administrators)
Admins manage the entire digital platform.
*   **What they can do**:
    *   Access the role-restricted **Admin Panel**.
    *   View all registered users and search by name/email.
    *   Change any user's role (e.g., promote a Builder to a Facilitator or Admin).
    *   **Manage the cards database (`/admin/cards`)**: edit cards, create new cards, toggle active states, and upload images with automatic resizing.
    *   Monitor system health.
