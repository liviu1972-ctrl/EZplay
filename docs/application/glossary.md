# Glossary of Terms — EZPlay

Welcome to the EZPlay dictionary! Here is the meaning of terms used across our educational ecosystem:

---

## 1. Virtual Tokens

*   **EZC (EZPlay Coins)**:
    *   The primary transactional currency inside the platform. It represents cash or liquid assets in a business context.
    *   Earned by participating in game sessions, completing business runs, and solving case challenges.
*   **EZG (EZPlay Gems)**:
    *   The rare progression currency. It represents intellectual capital, reputation, or strategic goodwill.
    *   Earned through outstanding leadership, mentoring, or reaching significant milestones.

---

## 2. The Founder Loop

The **Founder Loop** is the standard educational pathway a student follows to learn entrepreneurship:

1.  **Business Run**:
    *   A live or digital simulation round representing one fiscal year of a company. Players make choices on production, marketing, expenses, and track cashflow.
2.  **Founder Debrief**:
    *   A structured review session after the Business Run. Students sit down with facilitators or mentors to analyze their financial decisions (e.g., why did we run out of cash?).
3.  **Business Challenge**:
    *   A micro-case study or homework challenge proposed by a local business owner. Builders solve it using their business knowledge to earn extra EZC.
4.  **Level Up**:
    *   The milestone when a player gains enough experience (XP) to advance their competency level on one of the 5 perspective scales.

---

## 3. The 5 Competency Perspectives

*   **Market**: Skill in identifying customer demand, positioning the brand, and setting the marketing budget.
*   **Product**: Skill in product design, feature selection, quality control, and innovation.
*   **Operations**: Skill in team building, resource allocation, internal processes, and logistics.
*   **Finance**: Skill in reading balance sheets, cashflow management, profit margin optimization, and equity valuation.
*   **Strategy**: Skill in long-term planning, risk management, and competitive analysis under market uncertainty.
